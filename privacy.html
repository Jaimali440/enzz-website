Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who sees it, and how you can see, correct or delete it. Written to be read, not skipped.

Effective 2 October 2026  |  Version 1.0

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account, your shop, your learning or your job profile, and nothing we have no use for.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • If you connect Rafiki to WhatsApp or Telegram, we store which chat is linked to your account and process the messages you send so we can answer them.
  • Changes from chat, such as a sale, a restock or a price, only happen after you tap Confirm.
  • You can disconnect a chat, ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it. See how to delete your data.

1. Who we are

Enzz Kenya Limited. ("Enzz", "we", "us") is a Kenyan technology company based in Nakuru. We build a connected ecosystem of AI-powered products for businesses, learners and workers. For the personal data described in this policy, Enzz Kenya Limited. is the data controller.

You can reach us at support@enzz.co.ke, enzz@enzz.co.ke or +254 702 737 117.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Enzz Business / Biashara, for running a shop: sales, stock, credit, expenses, suppliers, purchase orders, online orders, promotions and shop profile.
  • Enzz Pata, for discovery: helping customers find businesses and businesses find customers.
  • Enzz Taalo, for learning: an evidence-based picture of what a learner can do.
  • AOIS, for work: verifying skill and matching workers with employers.
  • Rafiki, our AI assistant, including when you connect it to WhatsApp or Telegram.
  • Our website at www.enzz.co.ke and its contact form.

Not every product is live in every area yet. If a product described here is not available to you, the parts about it do not apply to you.

4. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and the Enzz product you use.
  • To answer your questions with Rafiki, for example "How much did I sell today?"
  • To prepare changes you ask for and carry them out only after you confirm.
  • To show a business on Pata, a learner's progress on Taalo, or a worker's profile on AOIS, in the ways you and your account settings allow.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse (such as limiting how fast messages can be sent), fix errors and improve reliability.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to send service messages such as security notices.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your information to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you without a person being able to review them.

5. Rafiki on WhatsApp and Telegram

You can connect Rafiki to a WhatsApp or Telegram chat so you can ask about your shop without opening the app. This is optional, and here is exactly how it works.

Linking

In the app you ask for a one-time link code. You send it to our business number on WhatsApp (or tap Start on our Telegram bot). The code is valid for 10 minutes and can be used once. We store it only in a protected, hashed form while it is valid. When it is used, we record that your chat is linked to your account.

What we receive

On WhatsApp we receive your phone number and the text messages and button taps you send to our number. On Telegram we receive your chat ID and username and your messages. We only handle one-to-one chats. We do not respond in groups, and we currently only read text.

What we do with your messages

We use your message to work out what you are asking, look up the information in your own account that is needed to answer, and send a reply. To do this, the message and the relevant shop information may be processed by an AI service provider acting on our behalf, under terms that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide the service to us.

Changes need your confirmation

Rafiki can prepare certain changes, such as recording a sale, restocking, changing a price, logging an expense or a credit payment, or acting on an online order. Nothing changes until you tap a Confirm button. We keep a record of each confirmed change so there is a clear answer to "who changed this?".

Third-party platforms

WhatsApp messages are carried by Meta through the WhatsApp Business Platform, and Telegram messages by Telegram. These companies receive and process your messages under their own terms and privacy policies, and not under ours. Please read them if you use these apps. WhatsApp shows a notice in the chat that the business uses a secure service from Meta to manage it.

Stopping

You can disconnect a chat at any time in the Biashara app, or by sending /unlink in the chat. After that, Rafiki stops answering in that chat.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these ways:

  • With the people you choose. A business listing on Pata is public. A worker's profile on AOIS is shown to employers according to the settings you choose. Taalo learner evidence is shared with the learner's teachers and, where applicable, parents or guardians.
  • Inside a business. A shop owner can see the activity of the employees they have added to their shop.
  • With service providers who help us run Enzz and are bound to protect your data: cloud hosting and database providers, AI processing providers, and the messaging platforms described above.
  • When the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid order from a court or authority, or to protect people from fraud or serious harm.
  • In a business change such as a merger or sale, in which case we will make sure your data stays protected and tell you if your rights change.

7. Our legal basis

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, we process personal data on one or more of these grounds:

  • Contract: to provide the Enzz product you signed up for.
  • Consent: for optional features such as connecting a chat app, and for showing your profile to others. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest: to secure and improve the service, and to prevent abuse.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share certain records.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and business records: while your account is active, and for a limited period afterwards so you can recover it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Link codes: expire after 10 minutes.
  • Chat links: until you disconnect. A disconnected link is marked as ended and stops working immediately.
  • Message identifiers used to avoid answering the same message twice: about 24 hours.
  • Action records of confirmed changes: kept for security and accountability even after an account is closed, and then deleted or anonymised when no longer needed. They contain the business owner's email and the details of the change, not your wider conversation.
  • Security and error logs: a short period, then deleted.
  • Records we must keep by law: for as long as the law requires.

9. How we protect it

We use measures suited to the sensitivity of the data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), signed and verified messages from WhatsApp and Telegram, one-time hashed link codes, rate limits against abuse, and checking your account's current role and status on every message so that a removed employee or suspended account loses access straight away. Chat requests can only come from a one-to-one chat that is linked to your account.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data in a way that is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • object to or restrict certain uses of your data;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, where we have no legitimate reason to keep it;
  • receive your data in a usable format and move it elsewhere;
  • withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before you withdrew.

To use any of these rights, email support@enzz.co.ke. We may need to confirm it is really you before we act. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at www.odpc.go.ke.

11. How to delete your data

Three ways to remove your data

1. Disconnect a chat app. In the Biashara app open Rafiki and go to the connect sheet, then tap Disconnect next to the chat. Or send /unlink in the WhatsApp or Telegram chat. This ends the link straight away.

2. Delete a chat from your own phone. Deleting the chat in WhatsApp or Telegram removes your copy only. Meta and Telegram keep their own copies under their own policies.

3. Ask us to delete your account and data. Email support@enzz.co.ke with the subject "Delete my data", from the email address on your account, and tell us which Enzz product you use. For WhatsApp, also include the phone number you message from. We will confirm by email once it is done.

When we delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except records we are legally required to keep and the action records described in section 8.

Deleting a shop owner's account also affects the employees linked to that shop, so we will confirm the request with the owner first.

12. Children and learners

Enzz Business, Pata and AOIS are for adults. Taalo is used by learners, many of whom are under 18. We only collect learner information through partner schools and teachers, or with the consent of a parent or guardian, and we use it only to help the learner and their teachers understand the learner's progress. We do not show learner information publicly and we do not use it for advertising. If you believe a child's data was given to us without proper consent, contact us and we will remove it.

13. Where data is processed

Our hosting and technology providers may process data on servers outside Kenya. When this happens, we take steps to keep your data protected in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, including contracts that require appropriate safeguards.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products grow, for example when we add new countries or new chat apps. When we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this version began.
