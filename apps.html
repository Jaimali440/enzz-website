Education · CBC Live preview

Enzz Taalo

Competence tracking, not grade tracking.

CBC asks a specific question: can this learner reliably do this, unaided, in more than one setting, right now? Most school apps still answer a different one — what fraction of the marks did they get right — and just call the result a "competency." Taalo doesn't average scores into a report. Every test question, assignment question and teacher observation is logged as one dated, contextualised piece of evidence, and each learner's status per competency is read fresh from that evidence trail — recent performance, how independently it was produced, and whether it's shown up in more than one setting — not a lifetime score.

CBC-aligned content

Assignments and tests mapped to Kenya's competency-based curriculum.

Recency over lifetime average

Status is drawn from a learner's most recent evidence, so today's status reflects where they are now, not where they started.

Independence-aware scoring

A perfect score with heavy teacher support can't count as "meeting" — how a result was produced matters as much as the result.

Two contexts for mastery

"Meeting" or "exceeding" needs proof from at least two settings — e.g. a written test and a classroom observation — real evidence of transfer.

Parent visibility

See what a child can reliably do — not just a mark on a single test.

AI-generated assignments

Save hours of prep time while keeping every learner challenged.

Gives urban and rural learners the same access to CBC resources — and a fair, evidence-based read of what they can actually do, not just a score.
