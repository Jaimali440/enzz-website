Find what you need at the shop next door — before you even leave home.

Pata is the customer side of the Enzz ecosystem: browse local shops, pharmacies, restaurants and bars near you, see exactly what they have and for how much, and order directly or by WhatsApp. No more walking to three shops to compare prices.

The everyday problem

Shopping around takes time you don't have.

Finding the shop with what you need, at a fair price, usually means walking around and asking — or just settling for the first place you find. Pata puts that search in your pocket instead.

Open the app, see what's nearby, check the price — then walk to the right shop, or just order it.

How it works

From "what's nearby" to "it's on the way" in four steps.

01

Set your location

Pata shows shops near you, and switching location takes one tap if you're somewhere new.

02

Browse a shop's full profile

See a business's products, prices and stock status before deciding to go there — no surprises.

03

Add to cart or message directly

Order in-app, or reach the shop on WhatsApp instead — whichever is easier for you.

04

Track your order

See everything you've ordered in one list, so nothing gets lost or forgotten.

What's next

AI comparison — coming soon.

Pata already lets you browse full shop profiles side by side. Next, the app will compare similar businesses for you automatically — so choosing between two nearby pharmacies or two produce stalls takes seconds, not a walk between both.

This feature is on the roadmap, not live yet.

We'd rather tell you clearly what's coming than blur the line between what Pata does today and what it will do soon.

Questions people ask

Common questions, answered simply.

Do I need to download an app to use Pata?
No — there's a web version you can try straight in your browser at pata.enzz.co.ke, alongside the app.
How do I pay for an order?
Payment happens directly with the shop, the same way it would in person — Pata connects you to them, in-app or via WhatsApp.
Is Pata connected to Biashara?
Yes — Pata is the customer-facing side of the same shop profiles businesses build on Enzz Business/Biashara, so what a shop lists there is what you see here.
