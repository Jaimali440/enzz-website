Run your whole shop by just saying what you want.

Biashara is built for the shopkeeper, pharmacy, bar or restaurant owner who doesn't have time to learn a complicated product. You don't dig through menus and settings — you tell the AI assistant what you want to do, and it takes you straight there. This page walks through exactly what that looks like, day to day.

View screenshots & features Watch how it works
The everyday problem

Going digital shouldn't mean learning a new job.

Most business products hand a busy shop owner a wall of menus, tabs and settings before they can log a single sale. That's fine if running software is your job — but your job is running a shop. Biashara flips that: instead of learning the product's structure, you just say what you need.

"Show me today's sales." "Add ten bags of sugar to stock." "Who still owes me money?" — say it, and Biashara takes you there.

How it works

From opening your shop to closing the till, in four moves.

01

Put your shop online

Build a simple profile — logo, banner, a short tagline — and your shop is discoverable in minutes, not days.

02

Ask the AI for anything

Type or say what you want in plain language. No menus to memorise — the assistant takes you straight to the right screen.

03

Scan instead of typing

Add and update stock by scanning products, so your numbers stay accurate without hours of manual data entry.

04

See the whole picture

Orders, customer credit, income and expenses — all in one dashboard you can check in seconds, not spreadsheets.

What it replaces

The notebook-and-guesswork way vs. the Biashara way.

Before
  • Stock counted by hand, or guessed when things get busy.
  • Customer credit tracked in a notebook — easy to lose, easy to forget.
  • No easy way for new customers to find or reach the shop.
  • Income and expenses only add up at the end of the month, if at all.
With Biashara
  • Stock scanned in and tracked automatically, with revenue and expected profit shown live.
  • Every customer's balance and partial payments tracked per person, per employee.
  • A shop profile customers can find and order from directly.
  • Finances — income, expenses, monthly summary — visible any time you check.
What's next

From navigation-speed to work-elimination.

Today's AI assistant is a friction reducer — it routes you to the right screen faster, but you still do the bookkeeping yourself. The next round of Biashara work turns that around: the system does more of the work, so the same owner produces more in the same time.

Photo-to-ledger for receipts

Photograph a paper receipt or forward a supplier's WhatsApp invoice image — line items, amounts and dates are extracted into a categorized expense entry for you to confirm, never auto-posted unconfirmed.

Voice-to-ledger for sales

A spoken note — even a plain WhatsApp voice note — becomes a logged sale with item, quantity, price and a running stock decrement. No typing, no menus.

Auto-drafted reorders

When a tracked item crosses a low-stock threshold, a reorder to the last-used supplier is drafted and pre-filled, ready for one-tap send.

One-tap financial snapshot

A clean income/expense summary and credit-repayment history, formatted for a lender or landlord — the direct product of a clean, photo-built ledger.

These features are on the roadmap, not live yet.

We'd rather tell you clearly what's coming than blur the line between what Biashara does today and what it will do soon.

Questions people ask

Common questions, answered simply.

Do I need to be good with technology to use Biashara?
No — that's the whole point. You say what you want in plain language, the same way you'd ask a person, and the AI takes you there instead of you learning a menu structure.
How much does it cost?
Biashara puts your business online for as little as 100 KES a month — built to be affordable for any entrepreneur, not just established businesses.
Can customers find and order from my shop directly?
Yes — your shop profile is visible to customers on Enzz Pata, the sister product, and they can order directly or reach you on WhatsApp.
What kind of businesses is it built for?
Shops, pharmacies, bars and restaurants — anywhere you're tracking stock, sales, customer credit and day-to-day finances.
