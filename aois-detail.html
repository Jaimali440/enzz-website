Work shouldn't depend on who you already know.

AOIS (formerly Enzz Fursa) connects employers with people looking for work — whether they hold a certificate or not. Post a gig, search for skilled workers, build a profile that shows what you can actually do, and get matched to real opportunities, including casual work through Vijana wa Mkono.

View screenshots & features Watch how it works
The everyday problem

Skilled, capable people get overlooked because they lack paper.

A lot of hiring still runs on referrals, or requires a certificate to even get a look — leaving out people who are genuinely good at what they do but never got formal training. AOIS gives both employers and job seekers a fairer, faster way to find each other.

Post the gig. Search the skill. Get matched — certified or not.

How it works

Two sides, one platform.

AOIS works differently depending on whether you're hiring or looking for work — here's what each side actually does.

Employers post gigs

List the work you need done, then search for and hire skilled or casual workers fast — no long back-and-forth.

Workers build a profile

Show what you can do — certified or self-taught — and get matched to gigs and jobs that actually fit.

Skill recommendations

Get pointed toward courses that boost your employability, matched to what employers are actually looking for.

A path for everyone

Vijana wa Mkono — work for people without formal certification.

Not every job needs a certificate. Vijana wa Mkono is AOIS's channel for casual, hands-on work — connecting people ready to work with people who need help done, while also opening a path toward getting certified for those who want one.

Questions people ask

Common questions, answered simply.

Is AOIS available to use right now?
AOIS is still in development. This page describes the plan and design so far — keep an eye on our channels for the launch.
Do I need a certificate to find work through AOIS?
No. AOIS is built for certified and unskilled workers alike, with Vijana wa Mkono specifically for casual work that doesn't require formal certification.
What was AOIS called before?
AOIS was formerly known as Enzz Fursa.
